Frequently Asked Questions

An ECTN (Electronic Cargo Tracking Note) is a mandatory cargo document required by several African countries before cargo arrival.

Common documents include the Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Freight Invoice and other shipment-specific documents, depending on the destination country.

You can apply online through our website or send your shipping documents directly to our email for review.
