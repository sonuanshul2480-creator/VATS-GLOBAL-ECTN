APPLICATION PROCESS

How It Works

Our simple step-by-step process helps exporters, freight forwarders and logistics companies obtain the required African cargo certificates quickly and efficiently.

OUR PROCESS

6 Easy Steps

From document submission to certificate issuance.

Step 1

Contact Us

Contact our documentation team by email, WhatsApp or through the online application page.

Step 2

Submit Shipping Documents

Send the required shipping documents including Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice and Packing List.

Step 3

Document Verification

Our experts review your documents and verify all shipment details before processing.

Step 4

Application Processing

We prepare and process your certificate application according to the destination country's requirements.

Step 5

Certificate Approval

After successful verification and approval, your cargo certificate is issued.

Step 6

Certificate Delivery

The approved certificate is sent to you by email so your shipment can proceed without unnecessary delays.
