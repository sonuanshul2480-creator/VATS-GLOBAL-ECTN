Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about African Cargo Certificates and our documentation services.

ECTN (Electronic Cargo Tracking Note) is a mandatory cargo tracking document required by several African countries before cargo arrival.

Countries including Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Congo, Ivory Coast, Gabon, Ghana, Guinea, Libya, Mali, Senegal, Togo and many others require certificates such as ECTN, CTN, BESC, BSC or FERI.

Generally required documents include Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List and Freight Invoice. Additional documents may be requested depending on the destination country.

Processing time depends on the destination country and the completeness of your documentation. Our team works to process applications as quickly as possible.

You can either email your shipping documents directly to us or use the Apply Online page to submit your application and upload documents.
