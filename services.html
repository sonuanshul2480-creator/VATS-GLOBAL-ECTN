African Cargo Tracking Certificates

We provide fast, accurate and reliable assistance for obtaining mandatory cargo tracking certificates required by African customs authorities before shipment arrival.

ECTN Certificate

Electronic Cargo Tracking Note (ECTN) is mandatory for many African countries. We ensure your certificate is issued quickly and accurately before cargo departure.

Togo, Burkina Faso, Niger, Guinea, Chad, Djibouti, Congo, Libya, Sudan, South Sudan and more.

Most African cargo certificates require the following shipping documents. Additional documents may be requested depending on the destination country.

  • Container Details

Company Information
